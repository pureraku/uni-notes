# Experiment 8: FCFS and SJF scheduling

## Aim
Compare First Come First Served and non-preemptive Shortest Job First scheduling.

## Compile and run
`cc -std=c11 exp08.c -o exp08 && ./exp08`

The four sample burst times are 6, 8, 7, and 3 time units. All processes arrive at time zero. The program reports waiting time, turnaround time, and averages for each method.
